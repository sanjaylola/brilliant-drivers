40-Hour Program

Beginner Driver Education Course

Ministry Approved Course Consists of Two Components

Online Instructions Component

30 Hours Of Online Digital Instructions

  • High-end digital course accessible on any device
  • Includes explanatory videos, animations, and quizzes
  • Based on Digital Curriculum Standards of the Ministry of Transportation
  • Complete online timebound support for content and technical issues by Ministry Licenced Driving Instructors

In-Vehicle Instructions Component

10 Hours of Intensive In-Vehicle Instructions

  • All instructors licenced by the Ministry of Transportation
  • Instructions provided in a calm, friendly environment by high-caliber professional instructors
  • Extensive practice on 3-point turns, parallel parking, hill parking, plaza parking, turns on small and wide roads, and multi-lane lane changes
  • Driving lessons planned to include night driving, freeway driving, and peak rush-hour multi-lane driving
