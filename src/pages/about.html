Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MTO certification, BDE coursework, and payment options.

Is Brilliant Drivers approved by Ministry of Transportation? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Brilliant Drivers is a Ministry of Transportation (MTO) approved Beginner Driver Education (BDE) Course Provider.

What is Beginner Driver Education (BDE) Course about? {{icon:chevron}}

BDE stands for Beginner Driver Education Course. It is a 40-hour course based on the Ministry of Transportation approved curriculum. You must successfully complete all components for successful completion of the course. Course completion information will be recorded on your 'Driver Licence History' issued by any ServiceOntario Office.

What are the benefits of completing the BDE Course? {{icon:chevron}}

After successfully completing the BDE course:

  • You become eligible to take your G2 road test after 8 months from the day of passing your G1 written test instead of waiting 12 months.
  • You may become eligible for an Auto Insurance discount upon successful completion of the course.
What is the course content of the BDE Course? {{icon:chevron}}

The BDE Course consists of a total of 40 hours of content:

  • 30 Hours Digital Instructions (Online)
  • 10 Hours In-Vehicle Instructions
What if I have a question on ONLINE BDE Course content? {{icon:chevron}}

For any question on course content, you may contact us by email or phone. Our qualified licenced teachers will contact you to answer your questions and clear your doubts.

Do I have to attend any classroom sessions for the ONLINE BDE Course? {{icon:chevron}}

No, you do not have to come for any classroom or in-person theory sessions for the BDE course. All theory content is online, and for in-car sessions we pick up and drop off at your home.

Can I pay in installments? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes, you can pay in installments—you can start any BDE package with $200. Call us at 416-276-2399 to learn more about our easy payment plans.
